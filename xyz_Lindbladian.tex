\section*{Boundary-driven anisotropic XYZ chain: Liouvillian superoperator ($N=2$)}

The dynamics $\dot\rho = \mathcal{L}\rho$ with
\[ \mathcal{L}\rho = -i[H,\rho] + \gamma_L\!\left(L_L\rho L_L^\dagger - \tfrac12\{L_L^\dagger L_L,\rho\}\right) + \gamma_R\!\left(L_R\rho L_R^\dagger - \tfrac12\{L_R^\dagger L_R,\rho\}\right), \]
\[ H = \sum_{j=1}^{N-1} \big(J_x\,\sigma^x_j\sigma^x_{j+1} + J_y\,\sigma^y_j\sigma^y_{j+1} + J_z\,\sigma^z_j\sigma^z_{j+1}\big), \quad L_L = \sigma^+_1,\quad L_R = \sigma^-_N. \]

In the Pauli-string basis $P_a = \sigma_{a_1}\!\otimes\cdots\otimes\sigma_{a_N}$ the superoperator is
$M_{ab} = \tfrac{1}{2^N}\,\mathrm{Tr}\!\big(P_a\,\mathcal{L}(P_b)\big)$ (rows $=a$, columns $=b$).
